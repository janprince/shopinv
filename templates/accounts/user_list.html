What each role can do

Owner

  • Everything a shopkeeper can do
  • See cost prices and profit estimates
  • Reverse completed sales
  • Add, edit and switch off users
  • Change shop settings
  • Read the audit history

Shopkeeper

  • Record sales and print receipts
  • Add and edit products, categories and suppliers
  • Receive stock and record damage, expiry, losses and returns
  • See sales and stock history
  • Cannot reverse sales, manage users or change settings
